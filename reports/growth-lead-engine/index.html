01Executive summary

SWK Properties will generate its own leads by earning attention in 622 cities with useful, local content and by making it effortless to opt in. Every lead is checked for consent, scored, routed to the right person and written into SWK OS with its channel, city and reasons attached.

What we decided

  • Consented capture, not scraping. Public posts that state buying intent become a prompt for a person to reply publicly. Nobody is added to the CRM unless they enquired or opted in. This protects the Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp and Google accounts the whole programme depends on, and keeps us inside UAE PDPL and the laws of each lead's country.
  • One engine, one source of truth. Normalise, consent-gate, dedupe, score and route in a single tested pipeline (33 automated tests) that writes into SWK OS through a low-privilege key.
  • Five seats only: Owner, Manager, BDM, IT Admin and one Agent, each with least-privilege permissions.
  • Volume from breadth. Twelve content pillars in 66 languages across 622 cities, 18 opt-in doors, and speed to lead.
  • No promises we cannot source. No guaranteed returns; ROI content is a worksheet that runs on the visitor's own numbers.
Three findings you should act on

1. SWK OS privilege escalation. Any OS role with write access on the admin module can mint an API key that acts as the Admin user. We reproduced it on a local copy: a role with that access exported every lead. IT therefore gets read-only admin access and only the Owner creates keys.
2. The single agent cannot see only their own leads in OS. OS has no row-level scoping for staff; the Agent can list all CRM leads.
3. A 09:00–19:00 agent shift misses the enquiry window in most cities. 47 of 71 Tier 0/1 cities have no overlap; a 14:00–23:00 shift cuts that to 10.

02Principles and boundaries

We doWe do not
Publish useful city-specific content and let people opt inScrape personal profiles, member lists or private messages
Listen to public posts with buying intent; a person replies publiclyAuto-DM or cold-call people who only posted publicly
Import exports where the person asked to be contactedBuy or rent contact lists
Store consent wording, time and URL with every leadMark anyone “Opted in” without evidence
Use claims that carry a source and a datePromise returns, prices or availability we cannot source

Scraping would breach platform terms (an account or ad-account ban ends the programme), collect personal data without a lawful basis, and produce leads who never asked to hear from us. The listening route gives visibility of intent without those costs.

03System architecture

The listening path is dashed because it never creates a CRM lead on its own. A person replies publicly, and the lead enters through an opt-in door when the person chooses to.

How 622 cities stay credible, not spam

  1. Tier 0/1 cities get localised content on their own tag (for example #KochiToDubai): 3 posts a week on the primary channel plus a monthly live.
  2. Tier 2 cities are served by language-cluster accounts plus a city page. A page must contain something genuinely local (currency framing, language, time-zone windows, real questions from enquiries). Pages that only swap the city name are not published.
  3. Every brief is generated from data, then reviewed by a fluent human before use.
  4. A city earns its own channel only when its results justify it at the 90-day review.

Rules

  • ROI is a worksheet. Visitors enter price, rent and costs in their own currency; the output is arithmetic. No appreciation forecasts.
  • Facts need a source and a date on the claims register. The property records in the repository are a snapshot, not live availability.
  • Every property ad carries the regulator's advertising permit number and QR code (confirm the current format with RERA/DLD).
  • Native-language review before publishing; right-to-left layout checked for Arabic, Persian and Urdu.
  • UTM convention: utm_source=<channel>&utm_medium=<organic|lead_ad|listing>&utm_campaign=<cityid>-<pillar>-<yyyymm>

A bug found only by testing against the real system

OS search (q) covers lead number, company and contact name only. The first version looked up duplicates with it, found nothing, and created a second CRM record for a repeat lead. The client now uses OS filters (filters=[{field,op,value}]) on phone, e-mail and the gid in the note; the repeat enquiry is merged and a note added.

Working notes, not legal advice. Counsel must confirm every row before launch and whenever a market is added. Sanctions and AML/DNFBP screening happen in SWK OS before any transaction, whatever the lead source.

Enforced in code

  • Consent gate: nobody who did not enquire or consent becomes a CRM lead.
  • Consent is never inferred; OS shows “Unknown” unless evidence exists.
  • Intake endpoints verify an HMAC signature, cap body size, rate-limit.
  • The ledger stores only hashed dedupe keys and OS ids, with owner-only file permissions.
  • Public-post signals keep a 280-character excerpt and are purged after 30 days unless converted.
